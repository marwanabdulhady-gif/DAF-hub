### Essential Question

How can you multiply mentally?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Three cyclists rode their bikes the distances shown in the table. Use mental math to calculate the total distance Pam and Anas each rode.

| Cyclist | Distance |
|---------|----------|
| Pam | 325 miles a month for 4 months |
| Anas | 25 miles a week for 8 weeks |
| Ghazi | 398 miles a month for 3 months |

You can use properties of operations to help multiply mentally. According to the **Commutative Property of Multiplication**, you can multiply in any order.

**Multiply 4 × 325** to find the distance Pam rode.

To multiply mentally, you can break apart numbers using place value and the **Distributive Property**.

```
4 × 325 = 4 × 300 + 4 × 25
        = 1,200 + 100
        = 1,300
```

Pam rode **1,300 miles**.

**Multiply 8 × 25** to find the distance Anas rode.

To multiply mentally, you can break apart and rearrange numbers using the Commutative and Associative Properties.

```
8 × 25 = (4 × 2) × 25
       = 2 × (4 × 25)
       = 2 × 100
       = 200
```

Anas rode **200 miles**.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: In the work for 4 × 325 above, why was 25 not broken into 20 and 5? Explain.

### Another Example — Compensation

Determine the distance Ghazi rode. Find 3 × 398.

400 is close to 398. Find 3 × 400 and adjust the answer.

3 × 400 = 1,200  
398 + 2 = 400, so 3 × 2 = 6  
Adjust the answer by subtracting 6.  
1,200 − 6 = 1,194

Ghazi rode **1,194 miles** in 3 months.

Some mental math strategies include both compensation and properties of operations.

### Problem Solving

11. Each elephant at a zoo eats 100 pounds of hay and 5 pounds of fruits and vegetables every day. How many pounds of food does the zoo need to feed one elephant for one week? Use mental math to solve.

12. Ali and 3 friends are planning a trip. The cost of the trip is SAR 599 per person. How much will the trip cost Ali and her friends? Explain the mental math strategy you used.

13. Kareem has a rock collection. On Monday, he found 16 new rocks. On Tuesday, he gave 9 rocks to his friends. After giving away the rocks, Kareem had 122 rocks left. How many rocks did Kareem have to start with?

14. **Critique Reasoning** Qasim used compensation to find the product of 4 × 307. First, she found 4 × 300 = 1,200. Then she adjusted the product by subtracting 4 groups of 7 to get her final answer of 1,172. Explain Qasim's mistake and find the correct answer.

15. **Higher Order Thinking** Do you think it would be better to use breaking apart and the Distributive Property or compensation to find the product of 5 × 328? Explain why and show how to find the product.
