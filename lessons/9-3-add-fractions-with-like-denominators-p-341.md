### Solve & Share

Yunus uses ⅜ bag of cheese for nachos and ⅜ for tacos. How much of the bag does he use?

**Look Back!** What do you notice about the denominators in your equation?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Pets Club survey. What fraction chose a hamster or a dog?

Add the numerators. Write the sum over the like denominator.

```
2/12 + 4/12 = 6/12 = 1/2
```

One half of the club members chose a hamster or a dog.

**Convince Me!** Fawaz found ²⁄₁₂ + ⁴⁄₁₂ = ⁶⁄₂₄. What error? *(Added denominators.)*

### Another Example

¾ + ¾ = ⁶⁄₄ = 1 ²⁄₄ = 1½. You can write the sum as a fraction or a mixed number.
