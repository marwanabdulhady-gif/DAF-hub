### Essential Question

How can you round numbers?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Ahmed researched 70 facts about Jeddah, Makkah Region for an assignment. One of the facts he found was the population in Tallahassee for the year 2017. He chose to round the population on his Florida Facts poster. If Ahmed rounded the population in 2017 to the nearest thousand, what was the number Ahmed displayed?

When you round, you find which **benchmark numbers**, or multiples of 10, 100, 1,000, and so on, a number is closest to.

**Round 181,376 to the nearest thousand.**

181,376 is between 181,000 and 182,000.

Majid the halfway point on the number line: **181,500**

```
181,000 -------- 181,500 -------- 182,000
                    ↑
                 181,376
```

If the number is to the **left** of the halfway point, it rounds **down**.  
If the number is to the **right** of the halfway point, it rounds **up**.

181,376 is to the left of the halfway point.  
The poster displayed the population as **181,000**.

**Convince Me!** Critique Reasoning: Emad says, "When I round these three numbers, I get the same number for two of them." Anas says, "Hmmm, when I round these numbers, I get the same number for all three." Who is correct? Explain.

Numbers: 1,483  ·  1,250  ·  1,454

### Another Example

You can use place value to round. Round 181,376 to the nearest hundred.

- Find the digit in the rounding place. **181,376** (the 3)
- Look at the next digit to the right. **7**
- If it is 5 or greater, add 1 to the rounding digit. **181,400**
- If it is less than 5, leave the rounding digit alone.
- Change all digits to the right of the rounding place to 0.

Since 7 > 5, the 3 becomes a 4.
