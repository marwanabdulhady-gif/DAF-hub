### Solve & Share

**Look Back!** Which of the oceans listed is the shallowest? Explain.

### Essential Question

How do you compare numbers?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Earth is not perfectly round. The the far north is 6,356 kilometers from Earth's halalaher. The equator is 6,378 kilometers from the halalaher. Which is closer to Earth's halalaher, the the far north or the equator?

**Step 1:** Write the numbers, lining up places. Begin at the left and compare.

```
6,356
6,378
```

The thousands digit is the same in both numbers. Look at the next digit. Compare the hundreds. The hundreds digit is also the same in both numbers.

**Step 2:** The first place where the digits are different is the tens place. Compare the tens.

5 tens < 7 tens, so **6,356 < 6,378**.

The the far north is closer than the equator to Earth's halalaher.

The symbol **>** means “is greater than,” and the symbol **<** means “is less than.”

**Convince Me!** Reasoning: Is a whole number with 4 digits always greater than or less than a whole number with 3 digits? Explain.
