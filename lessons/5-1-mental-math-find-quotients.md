### Solve & Share

Yusuf has 270 football cards to arrange equally in 9 boxes. Each box can hold the same number of cards. How many cards should Yusuf place in each box? Solve this problem using any strategy you choose.

**Look Back!** Reasoning: What multiplication equation could help you find the number of cards Yusuf should place in each box?

### Essential Question

How can you use mental math and place value to find quotients?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Mr. Diaz ordered a supply of 1,800 pastels. He wants to divide them equally among his class and 5 other art classes. How many pastels does each class receive?

You can use **basic division facts and place value** to divide.

**Find 1,800 ÷ 6.**

The basic division fact is 18 ÷ 6 = 3.  
18 hundreds ÷ 6 = 3 hundreds or 300.  
1,800 ÷ 6 = **300**

Each class will receive **300 pastels**.

If Mr. Diaz stores the pastels so each class will receive new pastels 5 times a year, how many pastels are handed out each of the 5 times?

**Find 300 ÷ 5.**

The basic division fact is 30 ÷ 5 = 6.  
30 tens ÷ 5 = 6 tens or 60.  
300 ÷ 5 = **60**

Each class will receive **60 pastels** 5 times a year.

Division is used to find equal groups.  
**Dividend ÷ Divisor = Quotient**

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: Write the missing dividends for equations such as __ ÷ 7 = 70, __ ÷ 8 = 50, __ ÷ 4 = 800. How did you determine each dividend?
