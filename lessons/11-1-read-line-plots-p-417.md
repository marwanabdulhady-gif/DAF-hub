Be precise; use appropriate labels.

**Look Back!** What other observations can you make?

### Visual Learning Bridge

A **line plot** shows data along a number line. Each dot (or X) above a point represents one number in the data set.

The numbers along the bottom are the **scale**.

Emad walked his dog (miles): data for 7 days. Line plot from ½ to 3.

- Most dots above 1 → most common distance is **1 mile**.
- Longest: **3 miles**. Shortest: **½ mile**.
- Difference: 3 − ½ = 2½ miles.

**Convince Me!** Write and solve an equation for *m*, total miles Emad walked in 7 days.
