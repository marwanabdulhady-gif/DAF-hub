### Essential Question

How can you use the Distributive Property to multiply two 2-digit numbers?

### Visual Learning Bridge

There are 25 teams in the Strike Out Club. There are 15 players on each football team. How many players are on all of the teams?

You can use an area model to show the **Distributive Property**.

**One Way — Break apart 15 into 10 + 5.**

```
25 × (10 + 5) = (25 × 10) + (25 × 5)
              = 250 + 125
              = 375
```

**Another Way — Break apart 25 into 20 + 5.**

```
(20 + 5) × 15 = (20 × 15) + (5 × 15)
              = 300 + 75
              = 375
```

**Another Way — Break apart both factors.**

```
        10          5
    ┌──────────┬─────┐
 20 │ 20 × 10  │20×5 │
    │   200    │ 100 │
    ├──────────┼─────┤
  5 │  5 × 10  │ 5×5 │
    │    50    │  25 │
    └──────────┴─────┘
```

200 + 100 + 50 + 25 = **375**

There are **375 players** on the teams.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: How does an area model illustrate the Distributive Property?
