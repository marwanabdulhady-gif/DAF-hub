### Essential Question

How can you model and solve multi-step problems?

### Visual Learning Bridge

A school uses 8 buses to transport the fourth-grade students and 6 vans to transport the third-grade students on a field trip. Each bus holds 24 students. Each van holds 15 students. How many fewer third-grade students than fourth-grade students are on the field trip?

You can use variables or letters to represent unknown quantities.

**Step 1 — Find and answer the hidden questions.**

Hidden Question: How many fourth-grade students are on the field trip?  
*F* = Number of fourth graders  
*F* = 8 × 24 = **192** fourth-grade students

Hidden Question: How many third-grade students are on the field trip?  
*T* = Number of third graders  
*T* = 6 × 15 = **90** third-grade students

**Step 2 — Use the answers to the hidden questions to answer the original question.**

Original Question: How many fewer third-grade students than fourth-grade students are on the field trip?  
*S* = the number fewer  
*S* = 192 − 90 = **102**

**102 fewer** third-grade students than fourth-grade students are on the field trip.

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: Ammar says she can solve the problem using the equation *S* = (8 × 24) − (6 × 15). Do you agree? Explain.

### Another Example

The Al-Harbi family gets 2 pizzas and 4 shawarma wraps at Eat-It-Up. What is the total cost?

Pizza SAR 18 · Shawarma SAR 6 · Chicken burger SAR 3 · Salad SAR 10 · Turkey Wrap SAR 9 · Drinks SAR 2

2 × 18 and 4 × 6 are **expressions**.  
*T* = (2 × 18) + (4 × 6) is an **equation**.

**Step 1** Write expressions to represent the hidden questions.  
What is the cost of 2 pizzas?  2 × 18  
What is the cost of 4 shawarma wraps?  4 × 6

**Step 2** Combine the expressions into an equation to find *T*, the total cost.  
*T* = (2 × 18) + (4 × 6)

**Step 3** Solve.  
*T* = 36 + 24 = **60**  
The total cost is **SAR 60**.
