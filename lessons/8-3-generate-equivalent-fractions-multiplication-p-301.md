**I can…** use multiplication to find equivalent fractions.

Librarian: ½ of books were nonfiction.

```
1/2 × 2/2 = 2/4
1/2 × 3/3 = 3/6
1/2 × 4/4 = 4/8
```

Multiplying by 1 (*n/n*) does not change the value.

Jad: *a/b* = (*n* × *a*)/(*n* × *b*), *n* ≠ 0. Agree.

Nawaf’s error: ³⁄₈ → (3×4)/(8×3) = 12/24 — multiplied by **different** numbers.

**Garden:** carrots ⅙; tomatoes ¼ = peppers ³⁄₁₂. Three equivalents for carrots.
