### Solve & Share

### Essential Question

How can you use equations to solve multi-step problems?

### Visual Learning Bridge

The students in the fourth and fifth grades are going to a concert. There are 178 students. There are 12 rows of 8 fifth graders. How many rows are needed for the fourth graders? (8 students per row)

To find the number of rows, you need to find the number of fourth graders. To find the number of fourth graders, you need to find the number of fifth graders.

Let *s* = the number of fifth graders, *f* = the number of fourth graders, and *r* = the number of rows of fourth graders.

**Step 1 — Find and solve the first hidden question.**  
How many fifth graders are there?  
12 × 8 = *s*  
*s* = **96**  
There are 96 fifth graders.

**Step 2 — Find and solve the second hidden question.**  
How many fourth graders are there?  
178 − 96 = *f*  
*f* = **82**  
There are 82 fourth graders.

**Step 3 — Answer the original question.**  
How many rows are needed for the fourth graders?  
82 ÷ 8 = *r*  
*r* = **10 R2**

Ten rows will be filled with 2 fourth graders left over. So, **11 rows** will be needed.

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: Does the answer of 11 rows make sense for the problem above? Explain.

### Problem Solving

5. **enVision STEM** How much more does it cost to generate 9 megawatt hours of electricity with conventional coal than with wind energy? Write one or more equations. Tell what your variables represent.  
It can cost SAR 105 to generate 1 megawatt hour of electricity with conventional coal. It can cost up to SAR 87 to generate 1 megawatt hour of electricity with wind energy.

6. **Model with Math** Anas earns SAR 8 an hour baby-sitting and SAR 6 an hour working in the garden. Last month, she worked 15 hours baby-sitting and 8 hours in the garden. How much more money does she need to buy a robot which costs SAR 199? Explain. Use one or more equations. Tell what your variables represent.

7. **Higher Order Thinking** Show two different ways to find the answer. Dog and cat food are sold in 20-pound bags. There are 14 bags of dog food and 12 bags of cat food on the store shelves. How many pounds of dog and cat food are on the shelves?

8. Fares needs SAR 858 to buy a computer. He has already saved SAR 575. He earns SAR 15 an hour tutoring and will tutor 12 hours next month. He can save SAR 8 a week from his allowance. How many weeks of allowance will it take Fares to save enough? Explain.
