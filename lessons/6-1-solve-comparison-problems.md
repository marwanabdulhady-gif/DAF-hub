### Solve & Share

Saud is making a square pillow with edges that each measure 18 inches long. She needs a strip of fabric 4 times as long as one edge of the pillow to make a border around the pillow. How long does the strip of fabric need to be? Solve this problem any way you choose.

Use reasoning to make sense of the quantities in the problem. You can use a bar diagram to understand the relationship between the quantities.

**Look Back!** Model with Math: How could a bar diagram help you write and solve an equation for the problem?

### Essential Question

How is comparing with multiplication different from comparing with addition?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Majid said the Rangers scored 3 times as many runs as the Stars. Jody said the Rangers scored 8 more runs than the Stars. Could both Majid and Jody be correct? (Stars scored 4 runs.)

**Compare with Multiplication** — Find 3 times as many as 4 runs.

```
Stars' runs:     [ 4 ]
Rangers' total:  [ 4 ][ 4 ][ 4 ]
```

Let *m* = the runs scored by the Rangers according to Majid.  
*m* = 3 × 4  
*m* = **12 runs**

**Compare with Addition** — Find 8 more than 4 runs.

```
Stars' runs:     [ 4 ]
Rangers' total:  [ 4 ][ 8 more ]
```

Let *j* = the runs scored by the Rangers according to Jody.  
*j* = 4 + 8  
*j* = **12 runs**

According to both Majid and Jody, the Rangers scored 12 runs. **Both could be correct.**

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: Describe when you would use multiplication and when you would use addition to make a comparison.

### Guided Practice

**Do You Understand?**

1. Use the information from the previous page. If the Rangers scored 5 times as many runs as the Stars, how many runs would the Rangers have scored?
2. Salman's football team has 4 times as many helmets as Emad's team. Emad's team has 21 helmets. Let *h* = the number of helmets Salman's team has. Complete the comparison sentence. Find the value of the variable.
3. There are 128 more trees in the park than at Ty's house. There are 3 trees at Ty's house. Let *t* = the number of trees in the park.

**Do You Know How?**

For 4–9, write a comparison sentence. For 4–7, find the value of the variable that makes the sentence true.

4. Karim has 6 times as many five-halalah coins as Sultan. Sultan has 18 five-halalah coins. Let *n* = the number of five-halalah coins Karim has.
5. Kareem has watched 238 movies. Jassim has watched 49 more movies than Kareem. Let *m* = the number of movies Jassim has watched.
6. Ammar tied 89 knots to make a macrame wall hanging. Hamza tied 3 times as many knots as Ammar. Let *k* = the number of knots Hamza tied.
7. Tareq sells 292 newspapers. Turki sells 117 more newspapers than Tareq. Let *n* = the number of newspapers Turki sells.
8. Turki has 48 markers. Sultan has 8 markers. Compare using multiplication. Write and solve an equation. How could you use addition to compare?
9. Luqman has 317 bottles. Karim has 82 bottles.

### Problem Solving

10. **Model with Math** Rashid swam 19 laps in the pool. Anas Majed swam 4 times as many laps as Rashid. How many laps did Anas Majed swim? Draw a bar diagram and write an equation to solve the problem.
11. **Critique Reasoning** Nawaf says the equation 600 = 12 × 50 means 600 is 12 times as many as 50. Jassim says the equation means 600 is 50 times as many as 12. Who is correct? Explain.
12. **Vocabulary** The amount that is left after dividing a number into equal parts is called the ________.
13. **Higher Order Thinking** A shirt is on sale for *d* riyals. The regular price is 4 times as much. Tareq has enough money to buy 2 shirts at the regular price. How many shirts can Tareq buy at the sale price? Explain.

### Assessment Practice

14. Select all the sentences that involve a comparison using multiplication.
- *k* is 26 times as many as 7.
- *u* is 18 more than 314.
- Tom ran 4 miles. Cindy ran 2 more miles than Tom. How many miles did Cindy run?
- Yusuf has 2 dogs and Jawad has 3 times as many dogs. How many dogs does Jawad have?
- Karim has 4 times as many pairs of shoes as her brother. Her brother has 8 pairs of shoes.

15. Select all the statements that can be represented by the equation 5 × 9 = *w*.
- *w* is 9 more than 5.
- *w* is 5 times as much as 9.
- Hamza sang 9 songs during practice. He sang 5 times as many as Yusuf, who sang *w* songs.
- Ghazi carried 9 buckets of water to his sister's baby pool. His mother carried *w* buckets, which was 5 times as many.
- Tom has 9 pens. Jad has *w* pens, which is 5 fewer.
