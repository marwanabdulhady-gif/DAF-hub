### Essential Question

How can you solve a problem involving multiplication as comparison?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Khalid and Salman are twins that attend different colleges. Khalid's college is four times as far from home as Salman's college. Khalid travels 192 miles to college. How far does Salman travel to college?

Multiplication and division have an **inverse relationship**.

Write a multiplication equation to find the number of miles Salman travels to college.

The number of miles Khalid travels, or 192 miles, is 4 times as far as Salman travels.

What number times 4 equals 192?

Let *m* = the miles Salman travels to college.

```
Khalid:   [ m ][ m ][ m ][ m ]  = 192 miles
Salman:  [ m ]
```

If 192 = 4 × *m*, then *m* = 192 ÷ 4.  
*m* = **48 miles**

Salman travels **48 miles** to college.

```
  4 ) 192
     −160    (40 × 4)
       32
      −32    (8 × 4)
        0
```

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: When do you use division to make a comparison?

### Guided Practice

**Do You Understand?**

1. Khalid travels 3 times as far to college as her friend Majid travels to college. Write and solve a related multiplication and division equation to find how far Majid travels to college.

**Do You Know How?**

2. Complete the comparison sentence. Find the value of the variable. If 3 × *m* = 48, then *m* = 48 ÷ 3.

For 3–8, write a comparison sentence and an equation. Find the value of the variable that makes the sentence true.

3. Kareem has 77 magazines. That is 7 times as many magazines Karim has. How many magazines, *n*, does Karim have?
4. Emad completed 75 math problems. That is 5 times as many math problems as Kareem completed. How many math problems, *m*, did Kareem complete?
5. Khalil counted 117 different colors at the paint store. That is 9 times as many as the number Ahmed counted. How many different colors, *c*, did Ahmed count?
6. Ali has 153 dominoes. That is 3 times as many dominoes as Sultan has. How many dominoes, *d*, does Sultan have?
7. Jassim practiced piano for *h* hours. His sister practiced for 12 hours, which was 3 times as many hours as Jassim practiced. Write and solve an equation to find how many hours Jassim practiced piano.
8. Majed practiced violin for 2 hours and her brother practiced trombone *t* times as long or 8 hours. Write and solve an equation to find how many times as long Majed's brother practiced trombone.

### Problem Solving

9. **Model with Math** Dawud is making soup that includes 12 cups of water and 3 cups of broth. How many times as much water as broth will be in the soup? Draw a bar diagram and write and solve an equation.
10. Turki wants to buy three light fixtures that cost SAR 168 each. He has SAR 500. Does he have enough money to buy the three light fixtures? Use a comparison sentence to explain your reasoning.
11. Majid has 4 times as many leaves in her collection as Jawad. Jawad has 13 more leaves than Ammar. Ammar has 10 leaves in his collection. How many leaves does Majid have in her collection? Explain.
12. **Higher Order Thinking** Jawad needs SAR 9,240 for her first year of college tuition. Each of her two grandfathers said they would match what she saves. She has 8 years before she goes to college. How much does Jawad need to save on her own each year to have enough for her first year with the help from her two grandfathers?

### Assessment Practice

13. Turki walked 20 miles for a fundraiser. Laith walked *m* miles. Turki walked 4 times as far as Laith. Which equation can be used to find *m*, the number of miles Laith walked?
- (A) *m* = 4 × 20
- (B) 20 = 4 × *m*
- (C) 20 = *m* ÷ 4
- (D) *m* = 20 + 4

14. Jassim and Rashid kept a reading log for the year. Jassim read 7 books and Rashid read 105. How many times as many books as Jassim did Rashid read?
- (A) 13 times  (B) 14 times  (C) 15 times  (D) 16 times
