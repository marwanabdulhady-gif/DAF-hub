### Essential Question

How can you estimate quotients using patterns and place value?

### Visual Learning Bridge

On “Clean Up Your Town Day,” 1,320 people volunteered to clean up the Springville parks. The volunteers were divided equally into teams to work in each of the town's parks. About how many people were on each team? (6 parks)

Multiplication and division are related. Mentally multiplying by tens or hundreds can help to estimate the quotient of a multi-digit division problem.

**Use Division Facts and Place-Value Patterns**

Find compatible numbers to estimate 1,320 ÷ 6.  
You know 12 ÷ 6 = 2, and 120 ÷ 6 = 20, so 1,200 ÷ 6 = 200.  
1,320 ÷ 6 is about **200**.  
There are about 200 people on each team.

**Use Multiplication Patterns**

Estimate 1,320 ÷ 6.  
6 times what number is about 1,320?  
You know 6 × 2 = 12, and 6 × 20 = 120, so 6 × 200 = 1,200.  
1,200 is close to 1,320.  
There are about **200 people** on each team.

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: Complete the calculations 4 × 100, 4 × 200, 4 × 300, 4 × 400. Explain how you can use the calculations to estimate 1,296 ÷ 4.

### Another Example — Rounding

You can use rounding to estimate quotients.

Estimate 357 ÷ 8 by rounding the dividend.  
Round: 400 ÷ 8 = 50  
So, 357 ÷ 8 is about **50**.

Estimate 5,582 ÷ 7 by rounding the dividend.  
Round: 5,600 ÷ 7 = 800  
So, 5,582 ÷ 7 is about **800**.
