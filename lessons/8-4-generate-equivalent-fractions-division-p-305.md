### Essential Question

How can you use division to find equivalent fractions?

### Visual Learning Bridge

In early May, Fairbanks, the Northern Borders Region, has daylight for ¹⁸⁄₂₄ of the day. What are some fractions equivalent to ¹⁸⁄₂₄?

A **common factor** is a factor two or more numbers have in common.

Two common factors of 18 and 24 are 2 and 3.

To find equivalent fractions, divide the numerator and the denominator by a common factor greater than 1.

```
18 ÷ 2 = 9     18 ÷ 3 = 6
24 ÷ 2 = 12    24 ÷ 3 = 8
```

¹⁸⁄₂₄, ⁹⁄₁₂, and ⁶⁄₈ are equivalent fractions.

¹⁸⁄₂₄ of a day is equal to ⁹⁄₁₂ of a day and to ⁶⁄₈ of a day.

**Convince Me!** Model with Math: Draw a number line and label it with equivalent fractions to show ¹⁰⁄₁₈ = ⁵⁄₉.
