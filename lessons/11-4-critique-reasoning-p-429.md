### Solve & Share

Snowfall 10 days. Nader said, “The difference between the greatest and least amount is 3 because the first measurement has one dot and the last has 4 dots.” How do you respond?

**Look Back!** Majid said total snowfall for 5 days was 10 inches. Is she correct?

**Thinking Habits:** What questions can I ask? Are there mistakes? Can I improve others’ thinking?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Fares compared two months of rainfall. She said February’s total was greater because ⅛ + ⅛ = ¼ and January’s highest was ⅛.

**Fares’s flaw:** She compared the **highest single days**, not the **monthly totals**. Add all values for each month, then compare.

January total vs February total; February had ⅛ inch more.

Badr thought January had more rain because it rained 7 days vs 5. **Flaw:** number of rainy days ≠ total rainfall.

### Practice

Khalil: “The height with the most dots is the greatest height.” **Flaw:** most frequent ≠ greatest value.

Nasser: 5 dots above ¾ hour so 5 students combined practiced ¾ hour. **Flaw:** 5 × ¾, not ¾.
