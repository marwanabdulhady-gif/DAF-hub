### Essential Question

How can you estimate quotients?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Majid wants to make 9 rubber band balls using about the same number of rubber bands for each ball. He bought a jar of 700 rubber bands. Estimate to find about how many rubber bands Majid can use for each ball.

There is more than one way to estimate a quotient. Majid does not need to know the exact number. An estimate is all that is needed.

**Use Compatible Numbers**

Estimate 700 ÷ 9.  
What number close to 700 is easily divided by 9?  
Try multiples of ten near 700.  
710 is not easily divided by 9.  
720 is 72 tens and can be divided by 9.  
720 ÷ 9 = 80  
Majid can use about **80 rubber bands** for each ball.

**Use Multiplication**

Estimate 700 ÷ 9.  
9 times what number is about 700?  
9 × 8 = 72, so 9 × 80 = 720.  
700 ÷ 9 is about **80**.

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: What compatible numbers can you use to estimate 132 ÷ 6? Why is rounding not a good way to estimate 132 ÷ 6?
