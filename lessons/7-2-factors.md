### Solve & Share

Jawad has 20 flowers. He wants to plant all of the flowers in equal rows in his garden. What are the different ways Jawad can arrange the flowers in equal rows? Solve this problem any way you choose.

You can use what you know about multiplying whole numbers to find equal rows.

**Look Back!** Make Sense and Persevere: How can you check you have found all the different ways Jawad can plant his flowers?

### Essential Question

How can you use multiplication to find all the factors of a number?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Jassim wants to arrange her 16 action figures in equal-size groups. What are all the ways Jassim can arrange her action figures?

```
1 group of 16 figures     1 × 16 = 16     So, 1 and 16 are factors of 16.
16 groups of 1 figure    16 ×  1 = 16

2 groups of 8 figures     2 ×  8 = 16     So, 2 and 8 are factors of 16.
8 groups of 2 figures     8 ×  2 = 16

4 groups of 4 figures     4 ×  4 = 16     4 is a factor of 16.
```

The factor pairs for 16 are **1 and 16, 2 and 8, and 4 and 4**.

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: How do you know there are no other factors for 16 other than 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16? Explain.

### Problem Solving

22. Ibrahim wants to list the factors for 88. She writes 2, 4, 8, 11, 22, 44, and 88. Is Ibrahim correct? Explain.
23. **enVision STEM** The roots of a plant are often the largest part of the plant. Winter rye can grow combined root tissue well over 984,000 feet in length. Write this number in expanded form.
24. **Model with Math** A restaurant receives a shipment of 5,000 ketchup packets. In one week, they use 1,824 packets. The next week, they use 2,352 packets. Write and solve equations to find how many ketchup packets the restaurant has left.
25. Any number that has 9 as a factor also has 3 as a factor. Why is this?
26. **Higher Order Thinking** A mother manatee is three times as long as her baby manatee.
    a. How long is her baby manatee? Write and solve an equation.
    b. If a blue whale is 9 times as long as the manatee shown, how much longer is a blue whale than the manatee? Write and solve equations.

### Assessment Practice

- (A) 1 × 9, 9 × 5, 3 × 15
- (B) 15 × 3, 9 × 1, 5 × 9
- (C) 5 × 9, 3 × 15, 9 × 5
