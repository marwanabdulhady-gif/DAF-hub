### Solve & Share

An artisan in Jeddah is laying decorative ceramic tiles for a courtyard floor. There are 13 rows with 15 tiles in each row. How many tiles are used in all? Use a grid or array to find the answer.

**Look Back!** How does breaking 13 into $10 + 3$ and 15 into $10 + 5$ make it easier to find the total number of tiles?

---

### Essential Question

How can you use grid arrays and partial products to multiply two 2-digit numbers?

---

### Visual Learning Bridge

A craft workshop is designing a carpet border that is 14 squares long by 16 squares wide. How many total squares are in the border?

You can draw an **array on grid paper** and separate it into tens and ones to find the four partial products.

**Find 14 × 16 = *t*.**

1. Break 14 into $10 + 4$.
2. Break 16 into $10 + 6$.
3. Find the four partial products:
   - $10 \times 10 = 100$
   - $10 \times 6 = 60$
   - $4 \times 10 = 40$
   - $4 \times 6 = 24$
4. Add all four partial products:
   $$100 + 60 + 40 + 24 = 224$$

So, $14 \times 16 = 224$ squares.

**Convince Me!** Why are there four partial products when multiplying two 2-digit numbers broken into tens and ones?

### Guided Practice

**Do You Understand?**

1. In the array for $14 \times 16$, which part represents $4 \times 6$? Where is it located in the grid?
2. How do place-value blocks connect to the grid array for $14 \times 16$?

**Do You Know How?**

3. Use the grid array to find $12 \times 14$.
   - $10 \times 10 = 100$
   - $10 \times 4 = 40$
   - $2 \times 10 = 20$
   - $2 \times 4 = 8$
   - Total = $100 + 40 + 20 + 8 = 168$

4. Use an array to find $13 \times 15$.
5. Use an array to find $15 \times 18$.
6. Use an array to find $16 \times 21$.
7. Use an array to find $14 \times 23$.

### Problem Solving

8. **Architecture** A courtyard wall in Al-Balad is covered with 18 rows of 15 decorative brass plates. How many brass plates are on the wall?
9. **Reasoning** Tariq found $14 \times 15$ by adding $100 + 50 + 40 = 190$. What partial product did Tariq forget to include? What is the correct answer?
10. **Higher Order Thinking** A mosaic border has 22 rows of 24 blue stones and 22 rows of 16 white stones. How many stones are there in total? Show two different ways to solve this problem.
