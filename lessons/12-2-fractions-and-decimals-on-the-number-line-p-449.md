### Solve & Share

Name the fractions and/or decimals of lettered points on number lines.

You can use structure: the number of spaces between whole numbers helps name each point.

**Look Back!** Is the name for point B different on a tenths line vs a hundredths line? Explain.

### Visual Learning Bridge

Emad skated 3.75 km (each lap 0.4? km). Locate ¾ and 3.75.

Number line 0 to 1 in tenths: 0.4 is four tenths of the way from 0 to 1.

Locate 3.75: between 3.7 and 3.8; a second number line from 3.70 to 3.80 in hundredths. Point at 3.75.

Mixed numbers and decimals can name the **same points**.

**Convince Me!** Which decimal on 0, 0.1, 0.5, 0.9, 1, 1.3, 1.8, 2.0 is not placed correctly?
