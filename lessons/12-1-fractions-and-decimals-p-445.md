### Solve & Share

7 out of 10 pet owners have a dog. Represent in a drawing.

**Look Back!** How many pet owners do NOT have a dog? As a fraction.

### Visual Learning Bridge

On Al-Andalus Street, 6 out of 10 houses have swing sets. Write as a decimal.

A **decimal** is another representation for a fraction; a number with one or more digits to the right of the **decimal point**.

Fractions with denominators of **10 and 100** may be written as decimals.

```
6/10 = 0.6     (6 in the tenths place)
6/100 = 0.06   (6 in the hundredths place)
60/100 = 0.60 = 0.6
```

Tenths place is immediately right of the decimal point; hundredths is right of tenths.

**Convince Me!** 75 out of 100 houses are two-story. Write as a decimal. Shade a grid.

### Another Example — Money

Cents are hundredths of a riyal. Amounts of money are written to the hundredths place. SAR 4.71 on grids.
