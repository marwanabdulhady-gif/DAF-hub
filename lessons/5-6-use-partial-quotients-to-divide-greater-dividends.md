### Essential Question

How can you use partial quotients to divide greater dividends?

### Visual Learning Bridge

A total of 277 people signed up to audition for a talent show. Five people at a time were brought in for a group interview. How many group interviews were needed to audition all 277 people?

Find 277 ÷ 5.

Divide until the remainder is less than the divisor!

**Estimate:** How many 5s are in 277?  
40 × 5 = 200  
50 × 5 = 250  
60 × 5 = 300  ← 60 is too many.  
There are at least fifty 5s in 277.

```
        50
         5
      ------
  5 ) 277
     −250
       27
      −25
        2
```

Partial quotients: 50 + 5 = 55  
277 ÷ 5 = **55 R2**

There were 55 group interviews with 5 people in each group. Two people remain. A total of **56 group interviews** were needed to audition all 277 people.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: How can you use multiplication and addition to check the answer above?  
(55 × 5) + 2 = 275 + 2 = 277 ✓

### Another Example

Find 1,968 ÷ 6.

```
         300
          20
           8
      --------
  6 ) 1,968
     −1,800
        168
       −120
         48
        −48
          0
```

300 + 20 + 8 = **328**
