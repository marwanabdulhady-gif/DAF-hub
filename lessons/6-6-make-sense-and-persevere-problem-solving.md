### Solve & Share

Ustadh Fawaz had her students design a snake house for the zoo. In the design shown, the anaconda has 538 more square feet than the python. The python has twice as many square feet as the rattlesnake. The anaconda viewing area is 1,928 square feet. How much of the house's area does each snake have? Solve this problem any way you choose.

**Thinking Habits**
- What do I need to find?

### Essential Question

How can you make sense of and persevere in solving a problem?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Badr and Ali have to buy their own instruments for the band. Ali's tuba costs 9 times as much as Badr's trumpet. Badr's trumpet costs SAR 159. Ali made SAR 1,025 from a fundraiser. He has a part-time job that pays SAR 8 an hour. How many hours does Ali need to work to have enough money to buy his instrument?

**How can I make sense of and solve this problem?**

I can
- identify the quantities given.
- understand how the quantities are related.
- choose and implement an appropriate strategy.
- check to be sure my work and answer make sense.

**What do you need to do to solve this problem?**  
I need to determine how much Ali's tuba costs and how much Ali needs to earn to buy the tuba.

Use the cost of the trumpet to find the cost of the tuba.  
SAR 159 × 9 = **SAR 1,431**  Ali's tuba costs SAR 1,431.

Find how much more money Ali needs to earn.  
SAR 1,431 − SAR 1,025 = **SAR 406**  Ali needs to earn SAR 406.

Find how many hours Ali needs to work.  
SAR 406 ÷ 8 = **50 R6**

Ali will not have enough money by working 50 hours, so he needs to work **51 hours**.

**Convince Me!** Make Sense and Persevere: How can you check to make sure the work and answer given above make sense?

### Guided Practice

**Make Sense and Persevere**

In the problem on the previous page, suppose Ali wanted to know how many weeks it would take him to work 51 hours. Ali works 3 hours a day and 4 days a week.

1. What are you asked to find and what is the hidden question?
2. Write and solve equations to solve the problem. Be sure to tell what each variable represents.
3. Does your answer make sense? Explain.

Think about this question to help you persevere: What's a good plan for solving the problem?

**Independent Practice**

The high school tennis team is selling tennis balls to raise SAR 500 for new equipment. They sell the balls for SAR 2 each. A case has 24 packages. Each package has 3 tennis balls. Will they make enough money if they sell 4 cases?

4. What are the hidden questions? Write equations to solve each.
5. Will the team make enough money? Explain.

### Performance Task — Designing a Flag

Rayan's group designed a flag for a class project. They used 234 square inches of green fabric. After making one flag, Rayan's group has 35 square inches of yellow fabric left. Twice as much green as orange. 3 times as much green as yellow. How can Rayan's group determine the total area of the flag?

6. **Make Sense and Persevere** What hidden question(s) do you need to answer first?
7. **Appropriate Tools** Draw diagrams and write equations to represent the hidden question(s). Be sure to tell what each variable represents.
8. **Be Precise** Use your drawings and equations to find the total area of the flag. Explain carefully, using the correct units.
9. **Make Sense and Persevere** What information was not needed to solve the problem?
