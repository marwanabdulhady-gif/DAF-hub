### Solve & Share

Turki wraps 3¼ loaves for a luncheon and 1¾ for a bake sale. How many loaves in all?

**Look Back!** How can you estimate the sum?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Badr has boards 2¾ ft and 1⅚? (dump: 2 ³⁄₁₂ and 1 ⁸⁄₁₂ style). Total length? How much longer is one than the other?

**Add with fraction strips:** add fractional parts; rename if ≥ 1; add wholes; combine.

Example: 2⁵⁄₁₂ + 1⁸⁄₁₂  
Fractional parts: ⁵⁄₁₂ + ⁸⁄₁₂ = ¹³⁄₁₂ = 1 ¹⁄₁₂  
Wholes: 2 + 1 = 3  
3 + 1 ¹⁄₁₂ = **4 ¹⁄₁₂**

**Subtract on a number line:** mark 2¾; move 1⅚ left. Difference as a fraction.

**Convince Me!** Boards 2⅓ and 1⅔. Total? Difference?

### Another Example

Number line: 1⅓ + 2⅔. Start at 1⅓; move 1 right twice, then ⅔.  
Fraction strips: 2⅔ − 1⅔ — cross out one whole and two thirds.
