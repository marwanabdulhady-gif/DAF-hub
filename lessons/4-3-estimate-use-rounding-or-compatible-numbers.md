### Solve & Share

Choose two factors from the numbers below to find a product that is as close to 1,600 as possible. Solve this problem using any strategy you choose.

What strategies do you know that can help you estimate a product?

**Look Back!** Construct Arguments: Why did you choose the two factors that you did? How do you know your factors will give the closest estimate of the product?

### Essential Question

What strategies can I use to estimate products of two 2-digit numbers?

### Visual Learning Bridge

The workers picked 14 dozen apples at Ms. Fares's apple grove and 12 dozen apples at Mr. Stuart's apple grove. There are 12 apples in one dozen. About how many apples did the workers pick?

14 + 12 = 26 dozen apples picked in total.

**One Way — Use rounding** to estimate 26 × 12.

Round 26 to the nearest ten → 30.  
Round 12 to the nearest ten → 10.  
30 × 10 = 300  
The workers picked about **300 apples**.

**Another Way — Use compatible numbers** to estimate 26 × 12.

Replace the factors with numbers that are close and easy to multiply.  
26 is close to 25. 12 is close to 10.  
25 × 10 = 250  
The workers picked about **250 apples**.

Your answer can be an estimate because an exact answer is not necessary.

**Convince Me!** Reasoning: Saud said she could find an estimate for 26 × 12 by rounding only 1 factor and multiplying 26 × 10. Do you agree? Explain.
