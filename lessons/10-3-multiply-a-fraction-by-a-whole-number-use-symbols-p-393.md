### Visual Learning Bridge

Sultan made 2 sundaes, ⅜ pint of ice cream each. How much ice cream?

You can use **structure** when multiplying a fraction and a whole number.

**One Way — unit fraction**

```
2 × 3/4 = 2 × (3 × 1/4) = (2 × 3) × 1/4 = 6 × 1/4 = 6/4 = 1 2/4
```

**Associative Property** of Multiplication.

**Another Way — multiply whole number and numerator**

```
2 × 3/4 = (2 × 3)/4 = 6/4 = 1 2/4
```

Sultan used 1½ (or ⁶⁄₄) pints.

**Convince Me!** Use properties to calculate 3 × ⅖.
