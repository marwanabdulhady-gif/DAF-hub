### Solve & Share

Dirt-bike track is ⅚ mile, divided into four sections. What is the length of the longest section?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Majed rides ²⁄₁₀ mile to pick up Majed, then they ride ⁵⁄₁₀ mile to the football pitch. Distance from Majed’s house to the field?

When you **add**, move **right** on the number line. When you **subtract**, move **left**.

```
2/10 + 5/10 = 7/10
```

The distance is ⁷⁄₁₀ mile.

**Convince Me!** Use a number line to find ⅜ + ⅜. Can you also find ⁶⁄₈ − ²⁄₈ on it?

### Another Example

Find ⁶⁄₈ − ⁴⁄₈. Start at ⁶⁄₈; move ⁴⁄₈ left. Ending point ²⁄₈.
