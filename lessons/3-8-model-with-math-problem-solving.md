### Solve & Share

Khalid took 120 color photos and 128 black and white photos on a field trip. Omar took 2 times as many photos as Khalid. How many photos did Omar take? Solve this problem using any strategy you choose. Use the bar diagram to help.

**Thinking Habits**
- How can I use math I know to solve this problem?
- How can I use pictures, objects, or an equation to represent the problem?
- How can I use numbers, words, and symbols to solve the problem?

**Look Back!** Model with Math: What representation did you use to solve the problem and show relationships?

### Essential Question

How can you represent a situation with math?

### Visual Learning Bridge

An art show uses 9 teams of art judges. If each team judges the work of 13 painters and 14 sculptors, how many artists attend the show?

**How can I model with math?**

I can
- use bar diagrams and equations to represent and solve this problem.
- decide if my results make sense.

Each team judges 27 artists (13 + 14).

```
number of artists for each team of judges
27  27  27  27  27  27  27  27  27
```

*a* = 9 × 27  
*a* = 243

There are **243 artists** at the show.

**Convince Me!** Model with Math: How could you decide if your answer makes sense?

### Guided Practice

**Model with Math**

Sultan's Stationery Store contains 1,219 boxes of cards. May's Market contains 3 times as many boxes of cards. How many boxes, *b*, does May's Market contain?

1. Explain how to use a picture to represent the problem and show relationships.
2. What equation can you write to represent the problem?
3. What is the solution to the problem?

When you model with math, you can draw a bar diagram and write an equation to represent the relationships in the problem.

**Independent Practice**

Anas has 6 albums of stamps in her stamp collection. Each album contains 440 stamps. How many stamps, *s*, does Anas have in her collection?

4. Draw a picture and write an equation to represent the problem.
5. What previously learned math can you use to solve the problem?
6. What is the solution to the problem? Explain why your solution makes sense.

### Performance Task — Hauling Fuel

A truck delivers a load of gasoline to a gas station 3 times a week. The storage tank at the gas station holds 9 loads of fuel. How much more gas does the storage tank hold than the truck?

7. **Make Sense and Persevere** What do you know and what do you need to determine?
8. What do you need to know to determine how much more the tank holds than the truck?
9. **Model with Math** Explain how to use bar diagrams and equations to represent the problem and solve.
