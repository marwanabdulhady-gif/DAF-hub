### Essential Question

How can you subtract whole numbers efficiently?

### Visual Learning Bridge

The movie theater already sold 172 seats. How many seats are still available? Seats 358 people.

Subtract 358 − 172.  
**Estimate:** 400 − 200 = 200

Here’s a way to record subtraction, called the **standard algorithm for subtraction**.

**Step 1** Subtract ones.  
8 ones − 2 ones = 6 ones.

**Step 2** Subtract tens.  
Regroup: 3 hundreds + 5 tens → 2 hundreds + 15 tens.  
15 tens − 7 tens = 8 tens.

**Step 3** Subtract hundreds.  
2 hundreds − 1 hundred = 1 hundred.

The theater has **186 seats** available.

The difference 186 is reasonable because it is close to the estimate of 200.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: How many times do you need to regroup to subtract 483 − 295? Explain.
