### Solve & Share

10 caterpillars: ¾, 1¼, ¾, 1½, 1, ¾, 1¼, ¾, ¾, ½ in. Plot. Equation for difference longest − shortest.

### Visual Learning Bridge

Ammar filled 20 water balloons; Badr 15. Line plots of weights.

- Who filled more? How many more? 20 − 15 = **5 more**.
- Ammar’s heaviest 2⅞ lb; Badr’s 2⅛ lb. Difference: 2⅞ − 2⅛ = **¾ pound**.

Each dot = 1 balloon. Farthest dot to the right = heaviest.

**Convince Me!** How much heavier was Ammar’s heaviest than her lightest? Badr’s heaviest vs lightest? Equations.

### Another Example

Snowfall 5 days. Total inches: add the values (with multiplicity). Most often: value with the most dots.

Use Commutative and Associative Properties to make adding easier.
