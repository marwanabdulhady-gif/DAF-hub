### Solve & Share

Shoe sales: complete the line plot. What length sold the most?

**Look Back!** Generalize: How can a line plot show the data that occur most often?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Sultan’s colored-pencil lengths (4 to 5 inches by eighths).

**Making a Line Plot**

**Step 1** Draw a number line. Choose a scale from least to greatest. Majid halves, fourths, eighths as needed.

**Step 2** Title the plot. Label what the numbers represent (include units).

**Step 3** Draw a dot (or X) for each data value.

**Convince Me!** Equation for *d*, difference between Sultan’s two shortest pencils.
