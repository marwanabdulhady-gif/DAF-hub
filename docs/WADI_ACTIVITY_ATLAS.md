## 00. Standing Invariants (Unchanged by Anything Below)

- **Printed numbers are locked:** All textbook figures come strictly from `uploads/`. Measured data belongs to the boys' own hands.
- **FIKR is the clock (Stages 0–6):** 40-minute period trim with dedicated pedagogical phases.
- **Lantern Drill $\times 2$–$\times 12$ runs every period:** Sitr (private mastery), not a public race.
- **Misbah never names a digit:** The lamp is a witness, not a grading engine.
- **AI is critic only, never author:** In Stage 4, the boy writes; AI probes completeness without solving.
- **No public leaderboards or ranking:** Collective progression via the class caravan pebble bowl.
- **Dojo P is a teacher clipboard, not a scoreboard.**
- **One self-contained offline file per door (`slides/door-X-Y.html`):** Zero external CDN dependencies.
- **Currency is SAR**, `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`. School leads: *Amm, Abu, Jadd, Amma, Khal, Khala, Jadda* (No *Umm*).

## 01. Six Research Findings That Changed Decisions Here

| Finding | Research Evidence | The Decision Forced on This Atlas |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 · CRA Sequence** | 2025 meta-analysis across 30 single-case studies reports a very large overall effect ($\text{Tau-BC} \approx 0.997, p < .0001$). Concrete first, then representational picture, then abstract symbol. | **Every door gets a hands-on twin, and the twin runs first.** The screen is the *representational* rung, not the concrete one. A door whose only manipulative is a mouse has skipped a vital developmental rung. |
| **R2 · Gamification Stability** | Meta-analyses show small-to-moderate math gains ($g \approx 0.44$, $I^2 \approx 87\%$). Mechanics only work when the game move *is* the mathematical reasoning. | **Points cannot carry a lesson.** Game moves must be isomorphic to the mathematical algorithm. Flames and streak pips scaffold pacing; real reward is the virtue named aloud (*sabr, amānah, ta‘āwun, istiqāmah*). |
| **R3 · Narrative Cognitive Load** | Serious game reviews warn that heavy narrative load during calculation exhausts working memory needed for mathematics. | **The Shahrazad ceiling:** Narrative hook strictly at Stages 0, 1, 4, and 6 (20–40 sec max). Stage 2 is the model board, Stage 3 is the interactive lab/slips, Stage 5 has zero story. |
| **R4 · Escape-Box Interdependence** | Escape-box studies show strong positive peer discourse and mathematical justification between teammates. | **Adopt at Gate scale, not door scale.** One Locked Gate challenge per topic (20 min revision block). Never weekly to avoid novelty decay. |
| **R5 · Three-Act Tasks** | Low-floor/high-ceiling tasks open with an image and no numbers; students generate the question before receiving data. | **Solve & Share Act 1:** Numbers stay covered for 30 seconds at Stage 1. Image first, question from the boys, then printed figures. |
| **R6 · Offline vs Web Manipulatives** | Digital tools (Polypad, PhET, GeoGebra) are web-hosted, violating the 1-file offline deliverable requirement. | **Two strict lanes:** *Wall lane* (teacher-projected, web-assisted) vs *Door lane* (built-in 2D zero-dependency engines running offline inside the standalone HTML). |

## 02. Four Kinds of Activity — One Legal Home Each

| Kind | Home on FIKR | Duration | What It Is | What It Replaces |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|---|
| **Hands-on Twin** | Stage 2, or first half of Stage 3 | 3–6 min | The concrete rung: tiles, strips, cups, bills, string, balance scales. The boy's hands execute the physical move before the screen. | A passive think-aloud the back row merely watches. |
| **Digital 2D Verb** | Stage 3 (and glimpse at Stage 0) | 8–12 min | The door's core 2D game engine (`airplane`, `bubbleshooter`, `treerings`, `mazechase`, `venn`, `cardsort`, etc.). | Silent worksheet drill. |
| **STEM Commission** | Stage 4 (once per gate); Stage 6 artifact | 1 door / gate | Real-world build or measurement whose data is printed nowhere because the boys generate it live. | A canned textbook word problem. |
| **Gamified Ritual** | Stage 1 and Stage 6 | Seconds | Pips, flames, the caravan pebble, the sealed page, and the virtue named aloud. | An artificial points economy. |

## 03. The Filter — Five Questions That Kill an Activity

1. **Nagrath’s Test:** Can he win the activity without doing the mathematical reasoning? *If yes, it is decoration — cut it.*
2. **Boarding-Pass Law:** Could two different doors swap this activity without anyone noticing? *If yes, it is generic — redesign it.*
3. **Does it invent a number?** *Printed numbers are locked. New data is legal only when measured by the boys.*
4. **Does it require internet in a boy's hands?** *Wall lane only — or rebuild it as an offline door verb.*
5. **Does it rank boys publicly?** *Out, whatever disguise it wears.*

## 04. The Open World Architecture

- **4.1 · The Rihla (Roaming Layer):** A sealed page does not close its gate. Lit gates remain accessible; re-entry opens the *Khan* (the side workshop behind the gate).
- **4.2 · Khan Tasks (Content Engine):** The same 2D game engine runs on new data the class brought back: hand spans, shadow lengths, water meter readings, recycled cans.
- **4.3 · The Unwritten Quarter (Endgame):** Blank on the valley map since Night Zero. Unlocks at 12 sealed pages into an open commission with no textbook answer: class water balance, campus shade map, solar oven.
- **4.4 · The Caravan (Collective Ledger):** One class caravan object advances along the valley as doors seal. Records doors, never individual boys.
